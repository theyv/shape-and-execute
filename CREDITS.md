## grilling

`skills/shape-and-execute/references/grill.md` adapts the interview mechanics of Matt Pocock's
`grilling` skill (Copyright 2026 Matt Pocock,
`github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling`, MIT License): the design
tree, the frontier asked whole in numbered rounds with a recommended answer, facts found by the
agent while decisions go to the user, and "done" as an empty frontier. It adds A/B/C answers
and writes each round's answers into the task files. The full MIT notice is in
[LICENSE](LICENSE).

## PROSE

The skill's structure follows PROSE, Daniel Meppiel's five constraints for agentic systems
(The Agentic SDLC Handbook, chapter 13,
`danielmeppiel.github.io/agentic-sdlc-handbook/handbook/ch13-the-prose-specification.html`).
No text is copied; the reasoning is in
[docs/adr/2026-10-06-follow-prose-without-ceremony.md](docs/adr/2026-10-06-follow-prose-without-ceremony.md).
