# Intent: Update notifications and model configuration

## Problem

Users need a way to learn about and receive updates to the viewer or the skill. Updates may
also introduce new model choices, raising the question of how those choices reach users and
their configuration.

## Goal

Make updates discoverable and usable for users of the viewer and the skill. Think through the
update experience before choosing how it works.

### Success means

- Users have a way to find out about relevant updates and act on them.
- The approach covers updates to the viewer and the skill.
- The approach addresses new model choices and their effect on user configuration.

## Proposed outcome

Explore options such as update alerts or a built-in update mechanism. Decide what should be
available, where users encounter it, and how updates are applied.

## Affected users and systems

Users of the viewer and the skill, including users with existing model configurations.

## Open questions

- Should the viewer or skill display update alerts, offer built-in updates, or use another
  approach? Should their update experiences differ?
- When a provider adds a model, should an update push model selections into users' configuration,
  ask users whether to change them, or handle this another way?
- How should existing user choices be treated when model options change?
